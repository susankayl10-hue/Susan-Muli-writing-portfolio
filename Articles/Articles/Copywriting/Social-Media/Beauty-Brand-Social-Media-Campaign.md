# Social Media Campaign — Beauty & Hair Brand

**Category:** Social Media Copywriting
**Campaign:** New Collection Launch
**Industry:** Beauty & Hair
**Target Audience:** Women interested in beauty, hair, fashion and self-care
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

---

## Campaign Objective

The goal of this campaign is to introduce a new hair collection, generate interest, encourage audience engagement and drive potential customers to make inquiries or purchases.

## Brand Voice

The campaign uses a tone that is:

* Confident
* Feminine
* Friendly
* Modern
* Conversational
* Aspirational

# Campaign Content Strategy

The campaign combines different types of social media content rather than relying entirely on promotional posts.

### Content Mix

**40% Engagement & Community**

* Questions
* Poll-style posts
* Conversation starters

**30% Educational**

* Hair-care tips
* Maintenance advice
* Styling information

20% Promotional

* Product launches
* New collections
* Calls to action

**10% Brand Storytelling**

* Behind-the-scenes content
* Customer experiences
* Brand values

This approach helps a brand maintain a balance between selling products and building relationships with its audience.
