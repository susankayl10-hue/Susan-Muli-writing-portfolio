## Savannah Cement Reaffirms Commitment to Kenya's Construction Industry Following Return to Market

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Athi River, Kenya — September 2026**

Savannah Cement Limited has reaffirmed its commitment to serving Kenya's construction industry following the company's return to the local market and the resumption of its commercial operations.

Based in Athi River, approximately 30 kilometres from Nairobi, Savannah Cement operates an eco-friendly cement grinding plant with an annual capacity of 1.5 million tonnes. The company says its focus remains on consistent product quality, customer experience, competitive value and a reliable distribution network.

### Strengthening Access to Cement Products

Savannah Cement currently offers cement solutions designed to serve different construction requirements, including Savannah 42.5R for specialised construction applications and Savannah 32.5R for general-purpose construction.

The company has positioned consistent quality and product availability as key elements of its value proposition as it serves contractors, developers, retailers and other participants across Kenya's construction sector.

### Customer Experience at the Centre

As competition within Kenya's construction materials market continues to evolve, Savannah Cement says it is focused on providing customers with a differentiated experience alongside reliable products.

The company's current operations include customer support and ordering services designed to help customers access information about its products, obtain technical assistance and place orders.

### Supporting Kenya's Construction Sector

Savannah Cement's return comes at a time when construction businesses and individual developers continue to require reliable access to building materials.

By maintaining its manufacturing presence in Athi River and strengthening its distribution footprint, the company aims to remain accessible to customers working on residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

The company has also highlighted its interest in environmentally conscious production, with its Athi River facility designed around green technology and environmental management.

### Looking Ahead

Savannah Cement says it will continue focusing on product consistency, customer service and availability as it builds its presence in Kenya's construction market.

The company is located in Athi River, Kenya, and provides customers with access to its cement products and related construction solutions.

### About Savannah Cement Limited

Savannah Cement Limited is a Kenyan cement manufacturer with an eco-friendly cement grinding plant located in Athi River. The facility has an annual production capacity of 1.5 million tonnes.

The company focuses on consistent cement quality, customer experience, value and distribution. Its current product offering includes Savannah 42.5R and Savannah 32.5R cement, alongside other construction solutions.
